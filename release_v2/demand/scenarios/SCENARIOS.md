# Perturbed demand scenarios S1-S4 (Comment 1.9)

Generated by `revision/tools/make_scenarios.py` from `sumo/turn_counts_edges.xml` (x1.0, 1355 vehicles in five 300-s intervals 0-300, 300-600, 600-900, 900-1200, 1200-1500 s) with the parameters in `scenario_params.json`. Every scenario has five realizations drawn with SUMO routeSampler seeds 2001-2005; the same seeds are used for every scenario, so that comparisons between scenarios are paired. ## S1 Directional imbalance

Definition: the through and left-turn counts of the two Svobody Street approaches (`nN_nC`, `nS_nC`) are multiplied by 1.3 in every 300-s interval; all other relations are unchanged. This represents a corridor-specific demand increase on the major street with unchanged side-street demand.

Parameters: factor = 1.3; affected relations `nN_nC->nC_nS`, `nN_nC->nC_nE`, `nS_nC->nC_nN`, `nS_nC->nC_nW`. Rounding: the affected cells (5 intervals x 4 relations) were rounded by the largest-remainder method so that their sum equals round(1.3 x 695 = 903.5) = 904; 8 cells were rounded up (ties to the earlier cell in file order). Every decision is in `S1/rounding_log_S1.csv`.

Result: 1564 vehicles (+209 vs x1.0); interval totals 324, 283, 345, 301, 311; left / through / right 262 / 1019 / 283. Turn counts: `S1/turn_counts_S1.xml`.

## S2 Platooned arrivals

Definition: the x1.0 counts per interval and relation are kept exactly, but departures are concentrated in a 40-s window of every 120-s cycle (the cycle of the field fixed-time plan), approximating platoons released by upstream signals. The Svobody approaches (`nN_nC`, `nS_nC`) receive their window at offset 0 s and the Prybuzka approaches (`nE_nC`, `nW_nC`) at offset 60 s within the cycle.

Parameters: window = 40 s, cycle = 120 s, offsets 0 s / 60 s, background density = 0.1 of the in-window density, 300 bins of 5 s over 0-1500 s. Method: the x1.0 turn counts were split by axis; routeSampler was run once per axis with `--depart-distribution` set to the binned density (Svobody: seed s, prefix `sv_`; Prybuzka: seed s + 50000, prefix `pr_`); the two outputs were merged and sorted by departure time. Expected in-window share: 40 / (40 + 80 x 0.1) = 83.3%.

Result: 1355 vehicles, counts per interval and relation identical to x1.0; achieved in-window share 82.1-83.4% over the five realizations:

## S3 Peak formation

Definition: each relation keeps its total over 0-1500 s, but the temporal profile is reshaped so that 50% of the vehicles fall in 600-1200 s (baseline 559 / 1355 = 41.3%). This represents a sharper peak inside the observed period with unchanged total demand and movement mix.

Parameters: peak_share = 0.5, peak = intervals i600, i900. Method, per relation: 0.5 x the relation total is spread over the two peak intervals and the remainder over the other three intervals, each in proportion to the relation's observed counts in those intervals; the five values are rounded by the largest-remainder method so that the relation total is unchanged (`S3/rounding_log_S3.csv`; profile fallbacks for relations absent from a block: 0).

Result: 1355 vehicles; interval totals 238, 214, 352, 324, 227; achieved peak share 676 / 1355 = 49.89%; left / through / right 231 / 841 / 283 (unchanged). Turn counts: `S3/turn_counts_S3.xml`.

## S4 Turning-proportion shift

Definition: in every 300-s interval and on every approach, the left-turn count is multiplied by 1.4 (rounded half up) and the through count is reduced by the same number, so approach totals and the total demand are unchanged. This tests sensitivity to a heavier left-turn share, the movement served by the protected left phases and the full-length left lanes of the network.

Parameters: left_factor = 1.4; left relations `nN_nC->nC_nE`, `nS_nC->nC_nW`, `nE_nC->nC_nS`, `nW_nC->nC_nN`. Cases where through would fall below zero (deficit taken from right turns): 0 (`S4/rounding_log_S4.csv`).

Result: 1355 vehicles; interval totals 280, 249, 292, 267, 267; realized left-turn total 325 (baseline 231, x1.407); left / through / right 325 / 747 / 283. Turn counts: `S4/turn_counts_S4.xml`.
