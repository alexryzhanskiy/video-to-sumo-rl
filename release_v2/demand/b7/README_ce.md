## Note on the one failing check (4 Nov 2026)

`x10/ce_rand/b7_ce_rand_x10_s7105.rou.xml` (draw 5): two vehicles of the 600–900 s interval (nN_nC→nC_nS and nN_nC→nC_nE) have depart = 900.00 s. routeSampler writes departures with two decimals, so a time just below 900 s is written as 900.00, and the check, which assigns t = 900.00 to the next interval, sees four cells off by one (600 s: −1, −1; 900 s: +1, +1). Relation totals and the file total (1386) are exact. The file is kept as generated: the seed is fixed by the preregistration (7101–7110), and the shift is 2 vehicles by less than 0.01 s.
